The counter is not counting

Prop your phone up so the front camera can see your hand, in reasonable light. The outline on screen shows when your hand is being tracked. If camera access is off, turn it on in Settings, GoonMaxxing, Camera.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel in Settings, your name, Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases.

Deleting your data

Everything is stored on your phone. Use Settings, Erase all my data, or delete the app.
