GoonMaxxing Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

GoonMaxxing is built so that we never receive your data. This policy explains what the app does on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not receive, store or share any personal information.

Camera

If you choose the camera counter, the front camera is used to detect the position of your hand so the app can count movement. Each frame is analysed on your device in memory and immediately discarded. Video and images are never recorded, never saved and never sent anywhere. The camera picture is not shown on screen. You can withdraw camera permission at any time in iOS Settings; the counter will not work without it.

Your session history

Session counts, times, scores and your settings are stored only on your device, in the app's protected storage. They are not uploaded and are not placed in iCloud by the app. You can delete everything at any time in Settings, "Erase all my data", or by deleting the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Support links

The Wellbeing screen links to third party support services. The app sends them nothing. If you call or open a website, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Age

GoonMaxxing is for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions: support@goonplex.com.
